# Animaciones de escalada y dash

Generadas con la herramienta integrada imagegen usando el atlas original de Gustavo como referencia de personaje. El PNG tiene fondo transparente y 16 cuadros: agarre, deslizamiento, salto desde pared y dash (cuatro por movimiento).

`movimientos.tres` conserva los recortes de cada cuadro como AtlasTexture editables en Godot. Las filas se recortan en y=0,330,660,980,1254; las columnas en x=0,314,627,941,1254. No modifica el atlas original ni las colisiones.

## Prompt utilizado

Create a production ready transparent PNG pixel-art sprite sheet for a Godot 2D side-scroller, using the attached image ONLY as CHARACTER AND PIXEL STYLE REFERENCE. Gustavo is the same stocky bald adult man, black full beard, white short-sleeved T-shirt, dark navy jeans, black sneakers with white soles, peach skin, black pixel outlines. Preserve his chunky proportions and simple pixel palette. NEW IMAGE, not an edit to that atlas. A SQUARE 1024x1024 sheet with EXACTLY 4 columns and 4 rows, 16 equal 256x256 cells. NO text, labels, borders, walls, backgrounds, floor, grids, UI, or other characters. Real alpha transparency throughout empty space. Every cell has ONE full body Gustavo with matching body scale, centered at x=128 in its cell, a nominal foot baseline at y=224, overall standing height about 180 pixels. Plenty of padding so no sprite crosses a cell. Row 1: 4 sequential WALL GRIP frames, facing RIGHT, BOTH hands reaching toward an invisible vertical wall on right, bent knees and toes bracing toward right; subtle breathing variations. Row 2: 4 sequential WALL SLIDE frames facing RIGHT toward invisible wall, hands high, bent knees, descending friction pose, a few tiny ochre friction sparks at hands and shoes only. Row 3: 4 sequential WALL JUMP frames travelling RIGHT, left hand pushes off an invisible wall behind on LEFT, forceful upward-right takeoff then tucked knees then recovery flight; consistent man identity. Row 4: 4 sequential HORIZONTAL DASH frames travelling RIGHT, torso low and leaning forward, fist tucked, legs swept back, energetic readable silhouette with short pale cyan pixel streaks trailing LEFT within same cell. Dash should look distinct from normal running. Crisp pixel clusters like a polished retro game, no painterly blur, no smooth vector art, no antialiasing. All 16 figures fully contained within their own cells. The deliverable is ONLY the game sprite atlas, transparent PNG.
